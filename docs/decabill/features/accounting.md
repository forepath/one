# Accounting

Decabill keeps double-entry books for the billing creditor (the issuer configured by `BILLING_ISSUER_*`): every business event is booked into a journal that follows the national standard of the issuer's country, bank and payment-processor statements are imported from files (or the processor API), and statements, VAT returns and statutory exports are generated for manual filing. DATEV exports are built from the same journal.

The feature is **off by default**. It only works when `BILLING_ACCOUNTING_ENABLED=true` **and** the billing issuer is complete (`BILLING_ISSUER_NAME`, `…_ADDRESS_LINE1`, `…_POSTAL_CODE`, `…_CITY`, `…_COUNTRY`). Otherwise all accounting routes return `404`, no accounting jobs run and the console hides the Accounting section. Country-specific outputs additionally need their identifiers (see [Country profiles](#country-profiles)).

## One ledger, tenants as a dimension

There is one set of books per deployment, because invoices are issued by one creditor. Every journal entry and line carries the **tenant** of the customer / supplier / bank account it belongs to, so:

- tenant-scoped pages (journal, account ledgers, open items, banking, statistics, per-tenant DATEV export) show the request tenant by default;
- another tenant or **all tenants** (`viewTenantId=<id>` / `all`) are only available to tenants on `TENANTS_ALLOW_GLOBAL_VIEWS`;
- entity-wide functions — chart of accounts, tax codes, fiscal years and locking, year-end closing, VAT returns, assets, exchange rates, match rules, processor sync, posting issues, statutory exports (GDPdU, FEC, SAF-T, XAF) — are reserved to allowlisted tenants (`403` otherwise). The capability flag `accountingGlobalAccess` tells the console.

Entity-wide entries (closings, depreciation, VAT payments) have no tenant and only appear in "all tenants" views.

## Bookkeeping methods

| Setting                        | Values                                     | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `BILLING_ACCOUNTING_METHOD`    | `accrual` (default), `cash`                | Accrual: balance sheet + P&L. Cash: the cash-basis profit statement (e.g. German EÜR) is the primary result. The journal is double-entry in both cases.                                                                                                      |
| `BILLING_ACCOUNTING_VAT_BASIS` | `invoice` (default, Soll), `payment` (Ist) | Output VAT in VAT returns by invoice date or by the cash received in the period (at the VAT share of the original invoice, so refunds give the VAT back and write-offs / discounts of unpaid amounts declare nothing). Input VAT always follows the invoice. |

## Chart of accounts

Charts are created from a **country template** or **empty**, and every account stays editable (number, name, type, statement position, automatic tax code, active flag, export codes). Full official charts can be imported from CSV (`number;name;type;position`) or a DATEV _Kontenbeschriftungen_ file (EXTF category 20).

- **Roles** (receivables, payables, bank, processor clearing, revenue per tax treatment, VAT accounts, fees, bad debt, inventory, COGS, depreciation, FX gain/loss, rounding, opening-balance accounts …) let the posting rules find accounts independent of numbering. A role belongs to at most one account per chart; a missing role produces a posting issue that names it.
- **Tax codes** define rate, VAT accounts, the VAT return boxes for base and tax (reverse charge: two tax boxes), EC-sales-list / OSS flags and the DATEV BU key.
- **Automatic accounts** (DATEV _Automatikkonten_) derive VAT from their default tax code; DATEV rows on them carry no BU key.
- Accounts with bookings cannot change number or type and cannot be deleted (deactivate them instead).
- **A chart can only change when a fiscal year is opened** (DATEV: _Kontenrahmenwechsel nur im Rahmen der Jahresübernahme_). Year-end closing maps balances to the new chart by explicit mapping, then by role.

Templates contain the accounts Decabill posts to plus the structural accounts of the statements, with Decabill's own labels. Copyrighted complete charts (DATEV SKR, Swiss KMU) are not bundled — import them if you hold a licence.

The country facts (rates, return boxes and formulas, filing channels and due dates, chart numbering, statement layouts, retention) were checked against official sources in November 2026. In those countries the books are a bookkeeping aid; invoices are not legally issued through the authority channels.

## Fiscal years and periods

- The first fiscal year starts at `BILLING_ACCOUNTING_START_DATE` (default: start of the current fiscal year; `BILLING_ACCOUNTING_FISCAL_YEAR_START_MONTH`, default from `BILLING_DATEV_FISCAL_YEAR_START_MONTH` or `1`). Later years are created automatically when the first booking needs them, inheriting the chart; open the next year manually to choose another chart or a short year.
- Each year has monthly **periods**. Locking a period is the GoBD _Festschreibung_ and is **irreversible**; periods lock in order. Submitting a VAT return locks all periods up to its end.
- Bookings dated in a locked period or closed year go to the first open period (the document date is kept). A posting holds a share lock on its period, so locking a period or closing the year waits for postings in flight, and postings arriving meanwhile move on to the next open period.
- **Year-end closing** carries balance-sheet accounts forward against the opening-balance account (DATEV 9000), receivables and payables **per open item** (9008 / 9009), and the result to retained earnings — each per tenant, so tenant views open with their own balances; the closed year becomes immutable. The figures are read under the period locks inside the closing transaction. On a chart change, balances map by explicit mapping, then by account role, then to an identical account (same number, type and name); the preview lists unmapped accounts.

## Journal

- Entries are balanced, numbered **gap-free per fiscal year** and **append-only** (database triggers reject updates and deletes). Corrections are reversal entries (`Storno`) plus a new entry.
- Zero-value documents (e.g. fully discounted invoices) book nothing.
- Automatic entries are **idempotent** per business event (`invoice:{id}:issued`, `settlement:{id}:payment` …) and are written **in the same database transaction** as the business change (inside a savepoint). A failing booking never blocks billing: it is rolled back to the savepoint and recorded as a **posting issue**, which the catch-up job (`BILLING_ACCOUNTING_CATCH_UP_INTERVAL_MS`, default 15 min) and the console's _Run catch-up_ retry.
- **Manual entries** reference accounts by number; a line with a tax code is entered gross and the VAT is split off.
- **Links**: every entry links to its source and any other document or transaction — invoices, credit notes, void documents, supplier invoices, settlements, payment attempts, refunds, bank lines, processor movements, stock movements, assets, VAT returns, other entries and **uploaded receipts** (PDF, PNG, JPEG, WebP, XML, max 20 MB). Each document shows its bookings (`GET /journal/by-link`). Links are added by **document number** (invoice, credit / void document, supplier invoice, offer, subscription, stock movement, asset or entry number), looked up within the entry's tenant; ambiguous numbers are rejected. The console shows links, counterparties, open items, match rules and posting issues by document / customer / supplier number, never by internal id (`GET /journal/reference-label` labels a single target). Links can be added to entries in locked periods but never removed there.

### What gets booked

| Event                                                                | Booking                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Invoice issued                                                       | Receivable (customer, open item) ↔ revenue per tax code + output VAT; OSS / EU lines carry country and VAT id; foreign currency at the ECB rate of the issue date |
| Invoice voided                                                       | Reversal of the issue entry (pre-start invoices: cancellation booked in full)                                                                                      |
| Partial credit note (withdrawal, config change, price recalculation) | Revenue + VAT ↔ receivable of the credited invoice, at the invoice's tax snapshot and exchange rate (the open item clears without a currency residual)            |
| Payment (processor webhook, auto charge, manual, bank match)         | Processor clearing / bank ↔ receivable; FX difference to FX gain/loss                                                                                             |
| Refund, payment reversal                                             | Opposite of the payment                                                                                                                                            |
| Write-off                                                            | Bad debt + proportional output-VAT correction ↔ receivable                                                                                                        |
| Customer credit applied to another invoice                           | Receivable (source open item) ↔ receivable (target open item)                                                                                                     |
| Supplier invoice issued / voided / paid                              | Expense or asset / inventory per line (account assignment), input VAT; EU / third-country services with reverse charge (input + output VAT); payable per open item |
| Stock withdrawal / return                                            | COGS ↔ inventory at moving average cost                                                                                                                           |
| Depreciation, disposal                                               | Depreciation ↔ asset account, one entry per month (a catch-up run never moves months into another period); remaining book value to disposal loss                  |
| Processor fees and payouts (API sync or report import)               | Fees ↔ clearing; payout clearing → cash in transit → bank                                                                                                         |
| VAT return payment                                                   | VAT prepayments ↔ bank                                                                                                                                            |

Open items from **before the bookkeeping start** are taken over automatically as opening balances (per invoice), with the amount that was open **at the start date** (gross minus the settlements dated before it, in the booking time zone, at the document's exchange rate). Settlements and voids after the start — including payments recorded before accounting was enabled (`migration` settlements) — are booked by the catch-up and clear those items. Enter or import the other opening balances (bank, equity, assets) as manual opening entries.

## Settlements (billing)

Balances and statuses of customer and supplier invoices are derived from **settlements** (`billing_document_settlements`): payment, payment reversal, credit, refund, write-off, cash discount and customer-credit transfers, each with its date and source. This makes "paid by cash" (`paid`), "settled by credit notes" (`credited`) and "written off" (`written_off`) distinguishable, enables **partial payments** (`partially_paid`), and keeps customer credit (negative balance) until it is applied to the next invoice or refunded. See [Invoices](./invoices.md#settlements).

## Banking

- **Financial accounts**: bank, cash, processor (clearing) or other; IBAN / account number, currency, ledger account number, owning tenant (or shared = global views only).
- **Imports**: upload a statement file or a ZIP of statements. Formats: ISO 20022 **camt.052 / camt.053 / camt.054** (all versions), **MT940 / MT942** (German DK `?xx` and Dutch structured `:86:`), **CODA** (BE), **Norma 43** (ES), **CFONB 120** (FR), **CBI RH** (IT), **BAI2** (US), **OFX / QFX**, **QIF**, and **CSV** with presets (Sparkasse CAMT-CSV / MT940-CSV, N26, Revolut Business, Wise, Qonto, PayPal, Stripe payout reconciliation, Mollie, Adyen) or saved custom mappings. The format is detected automatically or chosen.
- Statements are assigned to accounts by IBAN / account number **of the uploading tenant**; files for unknown or foreign accounts are rejected (a file is not proof of ownership).
- **Duplicates**: the exact file is rejected; transactions dedupe by bank reference (with booking date and amount, so generic references like QIF `ATM` or a fixed mandate reference do not swallow next month's line) or a fingerprint with occurrence counting, so overlapping statements only add new lines while identical lines on the same day survive. **Across formats** (the same period imported as camt.053 and as MT940 / CSV …, also within one ZIP), a line without a same-format twin is compared with the lines that came from other formats: same status, booking date, amount and currency, with no contradicting end-to-end id, counterparty IBAN or value date; identified lines pair first and each existing line absorbs at most one. Such duplicates are kept with the status _superseded_, pointing at the original line, and are never reconciled themselves. Booked lines supersede their pending (intraday / _vorgemerkt_) counterparts — matched by end-to-end id or IBAN, or by amount and date only when exactly one identifier-less pending line fits; superseded lines stay visible with the status _superseded_ so a re-imported intraday file cannot bring them back. Balance continuity is checked against the last closing balance; rolling back an import restarts it from the latest remaining statement and reopens the lines its lines had superseded (pending lines, cross-format duplicates — these get suggestions again). Lines a later overlapping import of the same format also contained were stored only once and leave with the rolled-back import; the rollback names those imports so they can be rolled back and uploaded again. An import records its target account, also when the account was detected from the file (IBAN, or German / Austrian bank code + account number).
- **Reconciliation**: suggestions from the ISO 11649 **RF payment reference** printed on every invoice (also in the GiroCode while accounting is enabled; without accounting the GiroCode keeps the invoice number as remittance text), invoice numbers in the remittance (as whole tokens: `INV-2026-1` never matches `INV-2026-10`), learned IBAN / text rules, amount + name, subset sums, and processor payouts. Unique high-confidence suggestions (`BILLING_ACCOUNTING_AUTO_MATCH_THRESHOLD`, default 0.9) are applied automatically. Matches settle invoices / supplier invoices; account bookings (fees, transfers) are posted directly on the transaction's tenant. _Remember counterparty_ (learned rules) is available to global views only, because rules steer suggestions and auto-matching for every tenant. A match may combine invoices and account bookings (e.g. a payment net of a fee). Invoices are entered by their number (looked up within the bank account's tenant); match rules name customers / suppliers by customer / supplier number. Matches can be undone (payments are reversed) and matched again, and imports rolled back while unreconciled.
- **Processor sync**: processors with a balance API (Stripe) sync charges, fees, refunds and payouts hourly (`BILLING_ACCOUNTING_PROCESSOR_SYNC_CRON`) into their processor account; charges and refunds already settled by webhooks are only marked reconciled (matched by direction, so a refund never counts as its original payment). A large backlog is paged over several runs without skipping older movements.

Upload security: size limit (`BILLING_ACCOUNTING_BANK_IMPORT_MAX_BYTES`, default 50 MB), XML without DTD / entities, ZIP entry / size / ratio limits without file-system extraction, linear-time parsers, encoding detection, CSV formula neutralization on the journal / trial balance CSV exports (the statutory GDPdU / FEC / XAF files keep the booked text unaltered, as the tax authorities' audit software reads them), raw files kept in file storage (`accounting/imports`, also in the File Explorer).

## Inventory valuation

Perpetual inventory at **moving weighted average cost**. A receipt's unit cost comes from the putaway (`unitCost`), the linked supplier invoice line (`supplierInvoiceId`), or the article purchase price; withdrawals leave at the current average and are booked as cost of goods sold. Supplier invoice lines referencing an article are booked to inventory.

## Fixed assets

Supplier invoice lines flagged _capitalize_ (or manual entries) create register entries. Classification: low-value assets up to `BILLING_ACCOUNTING_ASSET_GWG_LIMIT` (default 800) are written off immediately; with `BILLING_ACCOUNTING_ASSET_POOL_ENABLED` assets between `…_POOL_LOWER` and `…_POOL_UPPER` go into the German pool (1/5 per year); others depreciate straight-line monthly from the acquisition month. Depreciation runs book one entry per asset and month (the asset is locked while it runs, so concurrent runs cannot book a month twice); disposal books the remaining book value; the asset schedule (_Anlagenspiegel_) shows costs, additions, disposals and book values.

## Foreign currency

The ledger is kept in `BILLING_ACCOUNTING_FUNCTIONAL_CURRENCY` (default `EUR`). ECB euro reference rates are fetched on weekdays (`BILLING_ACCOUNTING_FX_CRON`, `BILLING_ACCOUNTING_FX_SOURCE=ecb|manual`); manual rates override them. Lines keep the original amount and currency (DATEV _Umsatz/WKZ/Kurs_ per row, FEC _Montantdevise_, SAF-T _CurrencyAmount_ / _ExchangeRate_, XAF currency); settlements book exchange differences. Inventory receipts from foreign-currency supplier invoices are valued at the invoice rate.

## Reports and statistics

Trial balance, account ledgers with running balance, open items with ageing, balance sheet (with previous year), P&L and the cash-basis statement per country layout. Every console page has a statistics lane (KPIs and charts): journal volume, revenue vs. expenses, sources, bank inflow / outflow and reconciliation rate, VAT payable trend, asset values.

## VAT returns

Returns are computed from the tax codes of the journal for a month, quarter or year, stored as drafts (recomputable, manual boxes such as DE Kz 39), then **submitted** with the filing reference (ELSTER Transferticket, portal receipt) — which freezes the figures and locks the periods — and optionally **paid** (booked). EC sales lists group by customer VAT id, OSS returns by country and rate. Filing itself happens in the authority's portal or through the tax advisor; no electronic submission (ERiC, MTD) is built in.

## Data retention

Journal rows cannot be deleted. Keep books, statements and the procedure documentation for the national period shown in the profile (DE: 10 years for books, 8 years for vouchers since 2025). Receipts and imported statement files live in file storage (`accounting/receipts`, `accounting/imports`).
