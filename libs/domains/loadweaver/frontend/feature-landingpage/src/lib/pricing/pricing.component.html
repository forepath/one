Nothing is closed

All features are public source. The commercial limitation comes from the license terms of each component, not from hidden code.

Public source, licensed per component

Every app and library carries its own LICENSE file. Single-site components are open source, multi-site components use the business license, and the line between them is visible in the repository.

AGPL-3.0: single-site

The node agent, the single-site control plane and the console basics.

BUSL-1.1: multi-site

Cross-site orchestration, multi-site storage replication, site failover, own-ASN BGP, multi-homing and anycast, and the fleet console. Free in production below €2M total finances per year, and every release converts to AGPL-3.0 three years after it ships.

Flat per tier

No node counting and no per-node license enforcement, so starting with one node and growing stays simple.
