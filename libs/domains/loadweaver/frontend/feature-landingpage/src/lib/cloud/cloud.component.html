The same product, without running it yourself

Loadweaver Cloud is not a shared platform. Each customer gets a cluster of their own, and it is a full Loadweaver cluster from the first node.

Your own control plane

Control plane, nodes and keys belong to your cluster alone.

Ready from day one

The first node already runs the control plane and the console, so your cluster is complete before the second node joins.

Grows like any cluster

Add nodes and sites whenever you need them, through plan and apply, bounded by your quotas.

More nodes, on your provider accounts

Your cluster orders further nodes itself, through plan and apply, with the provider tokens you configure in it.

  1. Your provider account

    Hetzner Cloud, DigitalOcean or another provider plugin, with your token

  3. Your Loadweaver cluster

    Orders the node through plan and apply, within your quotas

  5. A new node

    Joins the cluster, and is drained before it is ever deleted

Your own keys

Add your provider accounts to the cluster, or hand over the tokens when you order. With your own keys you pay the provider directly.

Kept as secrets

Tokens end up as Loadweaver secrets and are never shown in plans, logs or the audit trail.

Provider plugins

Hetzner Cloud and DigitalOcean out of the box, and more providers through dynamic provider plugins.

Leaving is data-safe too

Removing a node or cancelling the subscription follows the same rule as everything else: nothing is lost without your choice.

  1. 1

    Final backup

    Before teardown, a final backup goes to your external backup target, if you configured one.

  2. 2

    Read-only grace period

    You keep read-only access for export during a grace period.

  3. 3

    Only what we deployed

    We delete the server we provisioned, its IPs and DNS records. Nodes in your own provider accounts are not deleted; Loadweaver offers to decommission them or leaves them for re-use. Every step is audited.
