From a one-person business to ForePath

ForePath started in June 2016 with a simple idea. IT systems and software development from one hand, delivered by people who also run what they build. That combination has not changed since.

In 2022 the business became a limited company. A year later we co-founded a cloud start-up, and since 2025 we have shipped products of our own. Every one of these steps taught us something, and all of it flows back into the services we offer today.

From hands-on to AI native

We did not jump on AI overnight. We grew into it one layer at a time, and kept what made the work good in the first place.

  1. 2016

    Hands-on

    Every server, every script, every ticket by hand. We learned systems from the inside, the way only manual work teaches.

  2. 2023

    Automated

    Running Kubernetes platforms at VMMX made automation the default. Infrastructure as code, pipelines, and monitoring took over the repetitive work.

  3. 2025

    AI native

    Coding agents work in our delivery through Agenstra, and our instant quote runs a language model right in your browser. An engineer still reviews every change.
